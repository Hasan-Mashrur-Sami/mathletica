Welcome to Mathletica!

Mathletica is the Math Club founded for people who seek to embark on a journey with numbers: explore, question and deeply understand areas of mathematics. Our club helps members learn advanced math topics, engage in research, and support one another in developing stronger conceptual foundations. Mathletica also works to make mathematics accessible to everyone by providing free math education to underprivileged students in rural areas.

Resources

Mathletica encourages students to engage with the global mathematical community by participating in renowned events and competitions held each year.

  • International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO): The world championship mathematics competition for high school students, held annually in a different country. Learn More →
  • William Lowell Putnam Competition: A challenging mathematics competition for undergraduate college students in the United States and Canada. Learn More →
  • European Girls’ Mathematical Olympiad (EGMO): An international competition for high school girls, created to encourage and support young women in pursuing mathematics. Learn More →
